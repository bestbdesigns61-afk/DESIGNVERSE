THE STORY

Design is a sport.

DESIGNVERSE is the competitive creative universe built by BestB Designs — a place where designers don't just share work, they sharpen it in real challenges, earn votes and climb the global leaderboard.

DV
HOW IT STARTED

From sketches to a competitive universe

DESIGNVERSE began with a simple frustration: great designers rarely get real feedback, and great work too often disappears into the feed. We wanted an arena where skill is measured, progress is visible and every submission counts.

So we built a universe where you can publish designs, enter themed challenges, receive votes from the community and watch your profile grow — points, wins, followers and all.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

The values of the Verse

Everything on DESIGNVERSE is built around three ideas.

Competition sharpens craft

Constraints breed creativity. Real challenges push designers further than endless scrolling ever will.

Fair play, always

Every vote counts once, every submission starts equal and every rank is earned through the community, not bought.

Community over feed

We care about feedback, followings and genuine growth — not vanity metrics and noise.
